TASTE OF FREETOWN — MULTI-PAGE RESTAURANT WEBSITE

CONTENTS
- index.html   Home page
- menu.html    Food menu
- about.html   About the restaurant
- contact.html Contact information and enquiry form
- style.css    Shared styling for all four pages (responsive: phone, tablet, laptop)
- images/      Food and restaurant photos

HOW TO VIEW
1. Download the ZIP file.
2. Extract/unzip it.
3. Open the taste_of_freetown folder.
4. Double-click index.html. Use the navigation links to visit the other pages.

BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
- The Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp icons in the footer link to "#". Replace them with your real social media links.
- Contact details on contact.html: confirm the email address (tasteoffreetown@gmail.com) and opening hours (daily 10am-2am).
- Prices on menu.html are sample prices in new Leones. Replace them with your real prices and confirm the menu items.
- The contact form uses a mailto action. It opens the visitor's email application and depends on their device having email configured. For a professional live website, connect a form service or server-side form handler.
- Dish photos are in the images/ folder (jpg) and work offline. The About page restaurant photo is images/bright-restaurant.jpg.
- This is a static HTML/CSS website. Upload all four HTML files, style.css and the images folder together to your web hosting service to publish it online.
